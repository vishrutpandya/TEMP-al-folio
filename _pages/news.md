# News

Below are recent updates, announcements, publications, and events from the group.  
Any of the highlighted words can be turned into links — simply replace the `(#)`.

## **2024**

- **Our first SETI paper** based on the MSc project of *Priyatam Kumar* has been published in **MNRAS**.  
  It received significant media attention from  
  **[The Print](#)**, **[physics.org](#)**, **[NBC News](#)**, **[Sabine Hossenfelder](#)**,  
  **[Deccan Herald](#)**, **[New Scientist](#)**, **[EarthSky](#)**, and **[Business Today](#)**.

- We organized a course on **Numerical Radiative Transfer** (15–24 January 2024) at IIT Indore.  
  Instructor: **Prof. Ilian T. Iliev**, University of Sussex, UK.

## **2022**

- We organized a course on **Searching for Extraterrestrial Intelligence** (9–15 September 2022) at IIT Indore.  
  Instructor: **Dr. Erik Zackrisson**, Uppsala University, Sweden.

- We hosted a four-day international meeting (14–17 March 2022) on the **cosmic 21-cm signal from the Dark Ages to the present day**  
  under the framework of **SAZERAC**.  
  Meeting website: **[SAZERAC 21cm 2022](#)**.

- We organized a one-day international meeting on the **21-cm signal from Cosmic Dawn and the Epoch of Reionization**  
  under **SAZERAC**.  
  Meeting website: **[SIPS 2021](http://sazerac-conference.org/SIPS2021/3.html)**.
